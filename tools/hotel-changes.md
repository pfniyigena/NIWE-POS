# Hotel module — improvements HO-1 to HO-8 (v1.7.0)

Audit-then-build cycle for the Hotel module. All eight improvements chosen by the user. Migrations start at V26. Tests: `t69`+ (API), `walk52`+ (browser).

## HO-1 — Flow tests, paging, filters, exports, phone layout

- Before this step the only automated test touching Hotel was the licence gate (t26). `t69.py` (128 checks) now covers: room types/rooms, bookings with deposit and quote, overlapping and invalid bookings, availability filling up, check-in, extra charges and voids, payments, check-out refused while unpaid, walk-in, room move, early departure and extension, cancel with refund, no-show (only from the arrival day), maintenance blocking a room, night audit run twice (no double charge), dashboard, occupancy report, reservation chart, access rules (view / manage / none / not signed in).
- **Paging and filters** (same convention as the other modules: `page`/`size`, `X-Total-Count`; without `size` the old behaviour is unchanged, so pickers keep working):
  - `GET /api/hotel/bookings` — `status`, `q` (booking no., guest, phone, room), `from`/`to` (arrival date), `roomTypeId`, `source`; filtered in the database; newest arrival first.
  - `GET /api/hotel/guests` — `q` (name, phone, e-mail, ID number, nationality), alphabetical.
  - `GET /api/hotel/rooms` — `q`, `status` (OCCUPIED, VACANT, OUT_OF_ORDER, INACTIVE, or a housekeeping status), `roomTypeId`, `floor`.
  - `GET /api/hotel/maintenance` — `q`, `status`, `priority`, `roomId`.
- **Exports** (Excel, PDF, CSV; every row that matches, not one page): `/api/hotel/bookings|guests|rooms|room-types|maintenance/export`, `/front-desk/{arrivals|departures|in-house}/export`, `/reports/occupancy/export?from&to`. Same access as the screens (Hotel view); under `/api/hotel`, so the licence gate covers them.
- **Screens:** Bookings, Guests, Rooms and Maintenance use the shared search bar, pager and export buttons; Front desk, Room types and Report have export buttons (`hotel-export.component.ts`); the reservation chart is excluded from the phone card layout; `ListFilterComponent` got `extraActive` so "Clear filters" and "Export (filtered)" also react to a screen's own choice (room type, priority).
- Newer list requests cancel older ones (shared `ListState`), so a slow answer cannot overwrite a newer one.
- Tests: `t69.py` 128, `walk52.py` 46.

## HO-2 Seasons and rate plans (V26)

- **Seasonal rules** (Hotel > Rates): a name, a room type (or all), a date range, optional weekdays, minimum nights, a priority, and a price that is a fixed nightly price, a % change or an amount added/taken off. The highest priority wins; ties go to the most specific rule.
- **Rate plans** (e.g. Corporate −10%, Weekly −15% from 7 nights): a % and/or amount applied on top of the night's price. Chosen on the booking form or edit panel (only before check-in).
- **Per-night pricing**: a booking priced "Hotel rates" stores one price per night (`hotel_booking_night`). Folio charges, the night audit, invoices and accounting post each night at its own price. A booking with an **agreed nightly rate** stays on that rate whatever the rules say.
- Changing dates, room type, plan or room re-prices a hotel-rate booking; nights already lived are kept. Changing rules never changes existing bookings.
- **Live quote** on the booking form (night-by-night breakdown, tax, total) and `GET /api/hotel/quote`; **price calendar** per room type and plan (`/rate-calendar`, up to 92 days).
- Booking detail shows "Hotel rates / Agreed rate", the plan, and a "Price by night" table. Fixed a hazard in the old edit form, which would have turned every priced booking into an agreed-rate one.
- **Maintenance windows**: "out of service" can have from/until dates, so only those nights are blocked (no more blocking a room today for next month's repaint). Bookings already inside the window are listed as a warning and stay valid. The night audit refreshes out-of-order flags daily.
- Tests: `t70` (133 checks), `walk53` (27 checks).
- Known limits: the calendar prices one-night stays (so minimum-night rules show on bookings, not on the calendar); a room with its own rate ignores FIXED rules but follows % / amount rules.

## HO-3 Folio and policies (V27)

- **Numbered bills.** A stay can have up to six bills. Every charge and payment sits on one; bill 1 is the default. "Split bill" on the booking page adds a bill (optionally named and optionally billed to a company). Each charge has a Bill selector; "Room nights here" puts the room nights (existing and future) on a bill — typically the company's — while extras stay on the guest's. New extras, fees and Charge-to-room lines go to the first bill that is not a company's.
- **Bill to company (city ledger).** Only customers with credit enabled can be billed. At check-out, a bill sent to a company is not collected from the guest: it becomes an invoice (CL-00001…) on the company's account with the customer's payment terms as due date, after a credit-limit check (limit minus what the customer already owes in the shop and hotel). The guest ledger moves to the city ledger in Accounting (Dr Accounts Receivable "City ledger" / Cr "Guest ledger"). A bill with payments cannot be sent to a company, and payments cannot be taken on a company bill.
- **Company accounts** (Hotel > Company accounts): summary per company (billed, received, owing, overdue), invoice list with search, status (open/overdue/paid) and company filters, Excel/PDF/CSV, invoice PDF, and "Receive money" (partial or full; posts Dr Cash / Cr City ledger).
- **Move a charge to another in-house guest** (wrong room on the minibar): lines other than room nights and fees; both stays must be checked in; audit entry on both.
- **Fee policy** (Hotel > Settings): free cancellation until N days before arrival, late-cancellation fee, no-show fee, early-departure fee (all in nights of the booked rate), late check-out fee (% of a night after a set time), and whether fees carry the hotel tax. All default to 0 = no fee, so nothing changes until set. Fees are FEE lines posted as revenue; the held deposit pays the fee first and the rest follows the deposit choice (refund / keep); a fee not covered by the deposit stays owed and is paid with "Pay fee owed". Cancel, no-show and check-out dialogs show the fee beforehand with a "Waive" tick.
- **PDFs**: guest bill (whole stay or one bill), deposit / payment / refund receipt, company invoice.
- Tests: `t71` (152 checks, including "receivables on the ledger equal what companies still owe"), `walk54` (53 checks).
- Known limits: the company ledger is the hotel's own (it does not appear in the shop's customer collections / statements); a company bill cannot be voided once issued (receive money or correct it with an Accounting journal); fees are not taxed unless the policy says so.

## HO-4 Group bookings (V28)

- **Groups** (Hotel > Groups): a named group (wedding, conference, tour) with a code (GR-00001…), contact, dates, status (Tentative → Confirmed → Completed, or Cancelled), who pays, and an optional customer to invoice. Tentative holds rooms like a confirmed group does.
- **Room blocks**: N rooms of a type, over dates, at an optional group rate, with a **release date**. While a block is holding, the rooms the group has not yet booked are kept off general sale (the availability screens show Held). On the release date — or with "Release now" — the unbooked rooms return to sale; rooms the group already booked stay. A room freed by cancelling one member goes back into the group's block. A block cannot take rooms that are already booked or held by another group.
- **Rooming list**: the group's rooms are ordinary bookings (source "Group"), so check-in, charges, housekeeping and reports work unchanged. "Add rooms" books N rooms at once under the group contact on the block rate (or an agreed rate); paste a list of names to put guests on the unnamed rooms; edit a room's guest, phone, adults or room number; export the list to Excel/PDF/CSV. A member stay must stay within the group dates.
- **Who pays**: each guest (individual), the group pays the room nights (guest extras go on a second "Guest extras" bill), or the group pays everything. Billing can be changed until guests check in. Any single bill can also be sent to the group from the booking page ("Bill the group"); the booking page links back to the group.
- **Master account**: at each member's check-out, a group bill moves from the guest ledger to the group's account (Dr Accounts Receivable "Group ledger" / Cr "Guest ledger"). The organiser can pay a deposit (held, Cr Guest deposits) that is applied automatically as bills arrive, pay against what is owed (never more), and take back unused deposit. A group can be completed only when no room is left, nothing is owed and no deposit is held. Master statement PDF.
- **Cancel group**: cancels every room that has not arrived (cancellation-fee policy applies unless waived) and stops holding rooms; refused while a guest is in the house.
- Tests: `t72` (167 checks, including two groups competing for the same rooms and the books staying balanced), `walk55` (52 checks).
- Known limits: changing a block's rate does not re-price rooms already booked; the group account is the hotel's own (not in shop customer statements); a group has one contact guest used for unnamed rooms; no automatic e-mail to the organiser yet (HO-8).

## HO-5 Housekeeping v2 (V29)

- **Today's board** (Housekeeping > Today's board): "Prepare today's board" lists every room that needs cleaning — a **departure** clean (guest leaving today, or a vacant room marked dirty) or a **stay-over** service (guest staying on). "Share out the work" spreads the rooms over the active housekeepers by effort (a departure counts double a stay-over, least-loaded first); each row can also be assigned by hand. Start / Done record the time taken; Skip takes a reason; the day's tiles, per-housekeeper workload and an Excel/PDF/CSV export are on the same screen. Only today can be prepared or worked; earlier days are read-only history.
- **Departure rooms are protected**: a departure cannot be started or finished while the guest is still in the room. Checking out (or moving a guest to another room) puts the vacated room on the board as a departure clean and clears its do-not-disturb flag. Changing a room's status on the rooms grid keeps the board in step.
- **Do not disturb**: a toggle on occupied stay-over rooms. A DND room is skipped and cannot be reopened by hand; clearing DND puts it back on the list. The night audit clears DND, reopens the rooms that were skipped for it, and prepares the next day's board.
- **Inspection checklist**: Setup holds the inspector's lines (seven by default, editable, deactivate instead of delete). A supervisor inspects a cleaned room line by line; any failed line, or a note, sends the room back (task reopened, attempt counted, room DIRTY) and the failure note shows on the board. A pass marks the room inspected.
- **Lost & found**: numbered LF-0001…; room or place, found by, date; the guest who last stayed in that room that day is linked automatically (name and phone shown) so staff know whom to call. Hand over (who collected it), dispose, edit while waiting, delete; search, status filter and export.
- **Minibar**: item catalogue with price and tax flag (manage); "Charge to the room" posts the items used to the folio of the guest in the room at catalogue prices, tax as flagged. **Linen**: sent / back / damaged log per item with the balance still at the laundry (damaged counts as not returned).
- **Productivity** (Setup): per housekeeper over a period — rooms, departures vs stay-overs, average minutes each, inspections and how many were sent back.
- **Access**: working a room, inspecting, DND, logging a found item, minibar use and linen entries need Hotel view; planning (prepare, assign, share out), housekeepers, checklist, handing over / disposing / deleting items, minibar catalogue and linen delete need Hotel manage.
- Tests: `t73` (155 checks), `walk56` (44 checks).
- Known limits: no photos on lost & found items; minibar posts at catalogue prices only (no free-of-charge or complimentary lines); times are the server's, a housekeeper cannot back-date a finish; a room's cleaner name on the rooms grid is still free text for the old one-click flow.

## HO-6 Guest register (V30)

- **Guest profile** (Hotel > Guests > click a name): contact, nationality, ID, date of birth, profession, address, preferences and notes; tiles for stays, nights, **lifetime spend** (what the lived stays were charged, tax included, voided lines left out), average per stay and per night, first/last stay, usual room type and booking source, upcoming and cancelled; the guest's stay list with a link to each booking. Stays that were never lived (booked, cancelled, no-show) do not count towards spend.
- **VIP and blacklist.** VIP shows as a badge in the guest list, bookings list, front desk and booking page. A **blacklisted** guest (a reason is required) cannot be booked, cannot be added to a group's rooming list, and cannot be checked in on a booking made earlier; a new guest record with the same ID number or phone number as a blacklisted guest is refused too. Removing the flag clears the reason. Setting and clearing both flags is written to the audit log. Changing the flags needs Hotel manage.
- **Preferences** (high floor, extra pillow, late arrival...) are shown on the booking page, the front-desk lists and the registration card, and on the confirmation letter.
- **Duplicates and merge** (Guests > Find duplicates): guests are grouped when they share an ID number, a phone number (spaces and dashes ignored), an e-mail address or a name (case ignored); each group says what matched. "Keep this one, merge the rest" — or "Merge a duplicate into this guest" on a profile — moves the other guest's bookings, group contact roles, lost & found items and ID documents to the kept guest, fills in details the kept guest lacks, combines notes and preferences, carries over VIP and blacklist, removes the duplicate and writes an audit entry.
- **ID documents**: passport, national ID, driving licence, visa or other, as a PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB (up to 20 per guest); the file's first bytes must match its type. Optional document number and expiry date, with "Expires soon / Expired" flags. Download and delete from the profile; a guest with documents cannot be deleted until they are removed or merged. Stored in the database, not on disk.
- **Registration card** (PDF, any booking that is not cancelled): stay details, the guest's personal details (blank lines to fill in by hand when missing), purpose/coming from/going to lines, payment tick-boxes, terms and signature lines. **Confirmation letter** (PDF, for a booking waiting for the guest or in the house): dates with check-in/out times, room, guests, the price quoted for the stay, the deposit received, cancellation terms from the fee policy, and preferences.
- **Guest register** (Hotel > Guest register): everyone who slept in the hotel on a night of the period (arrival through the night before departure; today's house when no dates are given), one line per stay with name, nationality, ID, date of birth, address, profession, arrival, departure, room and booking; search, nationality filter, paging and Excel/PDF/CSV with a signature column. Uses the real check-in and check-out dates.
- Tests: `t74` (107 checks), `walk57` (43 checks).
- Known limits: the register lists one line per booking (the lead guest); companions are not recorded individually. "Coming from / going to / purpose of visit" are blank lines on the printed card, not stored fields. Documents are not virus-scanned and cannot be previewed in the page, only downloaded. Merging cannot be undone. The confirmation letter is printed or saved; e-mailing it comes with HO-8.

## HO-7 Reports and night audit (no migration)

- **Hotel > Reports** is now five tabs: **Day report**, **Occupancy** (the earlier report, unchanged), **By source**, **Forecast** and **Night audit**. Every tab downloads to Excel, PDF and CSV. Read access is Hotel view; the screen route is still admin-only as before.
- **Day report** (any day up to tomorrow; today by default): rooms in the hotel, out of order (maintenance blocks), sellable, occupied that night, occupancy %, room revenue, ADR and RevPAR; arrivals, departures, stay-overs, arrivals/departures tomorrow, guests in house now and what they owe; expected room revenue for today and later; revenue earned that day by type (room nights, extras, fees, restaurant, shop, room service) with tax; money taken by payment method (deposits, payments, refunds, net); month-to-date and year-to-date occupancy, revenue, ADR and RevPAR.
- **By source** (stays arriving in a period, up to a year): per booking source (blank = Unspecified) bookings, stays, cancelled, no-shows, % lost, room nights, room revenue, other revenue, ADR, average stay and average lead time; biggest room revenue first, with a total row.
- **Forecast and pick-up** (1-92 days from any date, default 30 from today): per night rooms, booked, held for groups, occupancy bar, revenue at each night's real rate (seasons, rate plans, extra-guest charges), arrivals, departures, and **pick-up** — nights and revenue on bookings made within the chosen window (1-90 days). Totals include ADR.
- **Night audit report** (today or an earlier day): night-audit status for the day and posting mode, in-house/arrivals/departures, room nights charged, everything charged by type, money taken by method, what the hotel posted to Accounting that day by account, and **six checks against Accounting** — cash taken that day; revenue posted and tax posted (all time); deposits held (bookings and groups); city ledger owed by companies; group ledger owed by groups. A check passes within 0.01. A red banner appears when any differ.
- Tests: `t75` (132 checks), `walk58` (46 checks, including a deliberately altered posting turning the banner red).
- Known limits: the day report's occupancy comes from stays that covered the night, so it can differ slightly from the occupancy tab (which counts charged nights). Revenue/tax/deposit/ledger checks are all-time balances, so an old difference keeps showing until it is fixed in Accounting. Cash check covers the hotel's own postings only (not POS sales). Source is the free-text field on the booking — "Booking.com" and "booking.com" are merged, other spellings are not. No comparison with last year.

## HO-8 Online booking, guest e-mails and calendar feeds (V31)

- **Public booking page** at `/stay` (no login), listed on the public home page as "Book a room" while the Hotel module and **Hotel > Online booking > Guests can book online** are on; otherwise the page and its API answer "not found". The guest picks arrival, departure, adults and children and sees only the room types that are really free for every night (maintenance blocks and group holds respected), cheapest first, with the full price (tax included), a night-by-night breakdown, rooms left and the deposit asked. Extra-guest charges and seasonal rates are the staff's own quote.
- **Booking**: name, phone (7-15 digits), optional e-mail and notes. Creates an ordinary booking with source **Website**, a unique 8-character **booking code**, and, when a deposit % is set, a **deposit due** and a **hold** (default 24 hours). Same name + phone reuses the same guest record. A blacklisted guest cannot book (same rule as the front desk). Protections: hidden form field for bots, a cap per phone number per day (default 3), a cap of 60 online bookings an hour, longest stay (default 14 nights) and how far ahead (default 365 days).
- **Deposit hold**: the guest is shown the deposit and how to pay (text set in Online booking). When the front desk records any deposit on the booking the hold ends. Every 5 minutes, and on "Release unpaid bookings now", online bookings with no deposit past the deadline are cancelled (no fee, reason "Deposit not received in time…") and the guest is told by e-mail if they gave one.
- **Look up and cancel** on the page with code + phone (digits compared, so spaces and dashes do not matter). Online cancellation is allowed only when nothing has been paid and no cancellation fee would apply; otherwise the page says to contact the hotel, so no refund or fee is ever decided on the web.
- **E-mails** (Online booking > E-mails; all off by default except that an online booking always sends its own confirmation): **confirmation** with the confirmation-letter PDF attached (online bookings at once; front-desk bookings if switched on, within 48 hours of booking), **pre-arrival reminder** N days before arrival (skipped for bookings made in the last 24 hours), **thank-you** after check-out (own message and review link), and a **release notice**. Each booking is marked once mailed so nothing goes twice; failures never stop a booking. "Send due e-mails now" runs the round on demand; booking page has **E-mail confirmation** for one booking.
- **Calendar (iCal) feed per room type** (Online booking > Room types and calendars): a secret address `/api/public/hotel/ical/<token>.ics` of all-day "Reserved" events for live bookings of that type (no names, phone numbers or prices); a type with a single room also shows its repair blocks as "Not available". "Make a new address" replaces the secret; "Switch off" removes it. The feed works even when the booking page is closed.
- **Staff screens**: Hotel > Online booking (settings, room types offered with a description, feed addresses); booking list and booking page show **Website** and **Awaiting deposit / deposit due by** badges and the booking code.
- Access: reading the settings needs Hotel view; changing them, feed addresses, "send/release now" and sending a confirmation need Hotel manage.
- Tests: `t76` (139 checks), `walk59` (57 checks).
- Known limits: deposits are paid outside the page (mobile money, bank) and recorded by staff — no card/payment gateway. Online bookings pick a room type, not a room; the front desk assigns the room at check-in. Rate plans are not offered online (the hotel's standard and seasonal rates apply). A partial deposit also ends the hold; staff chase the balance. The iCal feed is outbound only (it does not import bookings from other sites) and a multi-room type shows its bookings, not a count of free rooms. Pre-arrival mails skip bookings made less than a day earlier; reminders run every 10 minutes and need the mail server set up.
