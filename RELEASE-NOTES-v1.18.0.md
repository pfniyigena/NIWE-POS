# NIWE ERP, version 1.18.0

Installments: collections worklist, early payoff, payment reversal, default and write-off, statements and agreements. Versions 1.0.0 to 1.17.0 stay the base. One migration (V57). One new page (*Installments > Collections*), added by `tools/wire-v180.py`.

## What changed

1. **Collections page.** *Installments > Collections* lists the plans that need chasing, oldest overdue first. Filters: *Overdue* (default), *Not contacted this week*, *Promised* (a promise still to come), *Broken promises* (the promised date passed and nothing was paid since), *Due soon* (next instalment within 7 days) and *Defaulted*. Search by customer, phone, plan or invoice. Each row shows the amount overdue, how many days late, the next instalment, the last contact and any promise. *Log contact* records a call, visit or note, or a *promise to pay* with a date (today to a year ahead) and an optional amount. Sending a reminder or a statement by e-mail is logged automatically. The Installments overview warns when promises were broken or plans are in default.
2. **Early payoff.** On a plan, *Pay off early* shows a live quote: principal left, interest left, late fees left, interest waived and the amount to pay now. The waiver applies only to interest of instalments that are not yet due (*Installments > Settings > Interest waived when paid off early*, default 100 %; it can be changed per payoff). Receiving the quoted amount settles every instalment and closes the plan. If the amount changed since the quote was shown, the payoff is refused so nobody is charged a stale figure.
3. **Reversing a payment.** Each payment on a plan has *Reverse* with a required reason. Nothing is deleted: a negative payment and a negative invoice allocation are recorded, the accounting entries are reversed (cash and receivable, and the interest and fee income if any), and the original payment stays on the plan marked *Reversed* with who, when and why. A completed plan reopens. A plan that has been written off cannot have payments reversed.
4. **Default and write-off.** *Mark defaulted* flags a plan whose customer has stopped paying: it is listed under the Defaulted filter and gets no more late fees or reminders, but still accepts payments. *Back to active* returns it, and rescheduling a defaulted plan also returns it. Optionally (*Settings > Mark a plan defaulted when an instalment is this many days overdue*, 0 = never) the nightly job does it automatically. *Write off* (reason required) clears the unpaid principal from the invoice and books it as bad debt (Dr bad debt expense, Cr accounts receivable); interest and fees not yet paid are dropped. Written-off amounts are not counted as collected money in the daily credit-collection and customer-payment reports. A new account *Bad debt expense* is added to the chart (5230 in the Rwanda chart, 654 in the Chad chart); the starter-chart top-up adds it to existing databases.
5. **Statement and agreement.** *Statement (PDF)* prints the plan: terms, schedule, payments (reversed ones marked) and balance. *Agreement (PDF)* prints the instalment agreement with signature lines, using standard wording or your own (*Settings > Agreement wording*, one term per line). *E-mail statement* sends the statement, and optionally the agreement, to the customer's e-mail address. The standard wording is general; have it checked by a lawyer before use.
6. **Report.** *Installments > Reports > Defaulted and Written-off Instalments*, also available as a scheduled report. The agreements report now shows the outstanding balance of defaulted plans too.

## Technical

- Migration `V57__installments_v118.sql`: plan statuses `DEFAULTED` and `WRITTEN_OFF` (check constraint replaced), plan default and write-off columns, reversal columns on `installment_payment`, `reversal_of_payment_id` on `customer_payment`, new settings (payoff_rebate_percent, default_after_days, agreement_terms), new table `installment_follow_up`.
- Backend: `InstallmentService` (collections, follow-ups, `payoffQuote`/`payoff`, `reversePayment`, `markDefaulted`/`restore`/`writeOff`, `nightlyBody`), `InstallmentPrintFiles` (statement and agreement PDFs), `CustomerCreditService.reverseInvoicePayment` and `writeOffInvoice`, `AccountingService.postCustomerPaymentReversal`, `postInstallmentChargesReversal`, `postCustomerWriteOff`. New endpoints under `/api/installments`: `GET /collections`, `GET|POST /plans/{id}/follow-ups`, `GET /plans/{id}/payoff-quote`, `POST /plans/{id}/payoff`, `POST /plans/{id}/payments/{paymentId}/reverse`, `POST /plans/{id}/default|restore|write-off`, `GET /plans/{id}/statement-pdf|agreement-pdf`, `POST /plans/{id}/email-statement`, `POST /nightly/run`. All writes need the billing manage permission.
- Tests: `t107` (API, 135 checks), `walk91` (browser, 50 checks); full regression on the frozen tree.

## Not in this version

Automatic payment links, SMS reminders, bulk reminder sending from the Collections page, assigning plans to collectors, interest on defaulted balances.
