# Advertising on the Online Store

Sell space on your shop to other businesses. Staff keep **advertisers** (the businesses that pay) and their **ads** (a picture that links to the advertiser's web address). The shop shows the ads that are switched on and inside their dates, counts how often each is shown and clicked, and prints a report you can give the advertiser.

Open **Online Store > Advertising**. It has three tabs.

## Advertisers
Add the business (name, contact person, e-mail, phone, notes). Switching an advertiser off hides all of its ads at once. An advertiser that still has ads cannot be deleted: delete the ads first or just switch it off.

## Ads
For each ad choose the advertiser, give it a title (only staff see it), the link where a click goes (must start with `http://` or `https://`), the place or places it is shown, optional start and end dates, and the picture (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB; a wide picture about 1200 × 300 pixels works best). You can also note the **fee** charged, a note about it and whether it is **paid**. Nothing is invoiced automatically: these are notes for you and they appear in the report.

The three places:

- **Top of the shop home page**: a wide banner under the main picture of the front page.
- **Above the product list**: a banner above the list of products (the page where customers browse and search).
- **Order tracking page**: on the page a customer sees after placing an order, while waiting for payment and when the order is done.

An ad can be in one, two or all three. When a place has several ads they take turns (every few seconds, starting at a random one, so each gets its share), with small dots to pick one. Every ad is labelled **Advertisement**. A link opens in a new tab.

The list shows each ad's state: *Showing*, *Off*, *Advertiser is off*, *Starts on a date* or *Ended*.

## Counting and the report
- An ad counts as **shown** when at least half of it was on a visitor's screen. The same visitor connection counts once per ad and place for half an hour, so reloading the page does not add views.
- A **click** goes through the server, which counts it and sends the visitor to the link you saved (a visitor cannot change where it goes). Repeated clicks within ten seconds count once.
- Counts are kept per day and place. The **Report** tab shows, for a period and optionally one advertiser, the views, clicks and click rate of each ad with the figures per place, plus the fee and whether it is paid. **Print** opens a clean page to print or save as PDF; **Download CSV** gives the same rows for a spreadsheet.

## Good to know
- Ad blockers may hide ads or the counting for some visitors, so the numbers are a little lower than the real ones. Say so to advertisers.
- Only the shop's own staff can add ads and see the counts. Deleting an ad deletes its counts.
- Staff need the same permission as the rest of the Online Store (Operations area).
- French texts are included. The ads themselves are pictures, so write the text on them in the languages your customers use.
