# The driver's cash balance (v1.60.0)

## Where
On the driver's private page, the card **Cash I hold** above the deliveries.

## What it shows
- Total cash taken at the door and not handed in yet.
- One line per order: code, customer, date, amount.
- **Handed in lately** (tap to open): the last ten settlements, amount handed in and the difference.

## How it moves
1. The driver hands an order over with the customer's code and the cash was collected: the order joins "Cash I hold".
2. He hands the cash to the shop. Staff open Online Store > Driver cash and settle the order with the amount really received.
3. The order leaves "Cash I hold" and shows under "Handed in lately" with the difference, if any.

The driver cannot settle anything himself, and sees no other driver's cash.
