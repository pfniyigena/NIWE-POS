# A new delivery time after a failed visit (v1.64.0)

Needs **Shop settings > Delivery times** switched on.

1. Staff press **Delivery failed** with the reason. The order goes back to Packed and its window is freed.
2. The customer's message asks him to open his order page. There he picks a day and a window and confirms.
3. Staff see the new window on the order (and a notice by e-mail/text). Until then the order shows "Waiting for a new time".
4. Then send it out again. If windows are compulsory, the order needs a window first; **Change time** sets one when the customer cannot.

Good to know: a full or too-soon window cannot be chosen; the customer can change his choice until the order goes out; with windows off nothing changes (staff phone the customer).
