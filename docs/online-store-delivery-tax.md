# Tax on the delivery fee (v1.59.1)

## Set it up
Online Store > Shop settings > Delivery areas. For each area choose the **Tax** from your existing taxes, then **Save**. For a new area, choose it before **Add area**.

## How it works
- The fee of the area is what the customer pays. The chosen tax is included in it (the part of the fee that is tax is worked out from the rate).
- When staff accept an order, the fee goes on the sale order as one line of the delivery product, carrying the area's tax as an included tax. The invoice and the cash collected then match the order total.
- Without a choice, the delivery product's own tax is used (this is the old behaviour). If that tax is added on top, the invoice is higher than the order total: choose a tax on the area, or set the product's tax as included.

## Good to know
- The tax is copied onto each order when it is placed: editing an area later changes only new orders.
- A tax that is switched off cannot be chosen for an area, but an area that already uses it keeps it.
- Tax reports and the tax account get the tax part of the fee like any other line.
