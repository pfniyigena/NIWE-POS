# Delivery time slots (v1.61.0)

## Set it up
Online Store > Shop settings > **Delivery times**.

1. Add the windows you deliver in (From, To, orders per day, weekdays) with **Add window**.
2. Tick **Customers choose a delivery time**, set **Days ahead** and **Notice**, then **Save**.
3. Add **days without deliveries** (holidays) if needed.
4. Optional: tick **They must choose one** to remove "As soon as possible".

## What the customer sees
At checkout, with Delivery chosen: **Delivery time**. A day list, then the windows of that day. Full or too-late windows are greyed out. The choice is shown on the order page and in the messages.

## What staff do
- Online orders shows the window; **Change time** moves the order (even to a full window) or back to As soon as possible. The customer is told.
- The driver's page, the delivery slip and the run sheet show the window; the run sheet is sorted by window.

## Good to know
- Rejected and cancelled orders free their place.
- Lowering a limit never removes orders that are already booked.
- Removing a window does not change orders already placed in it (they keep their time).
- The time zone of the server decides "today" and "too late".
