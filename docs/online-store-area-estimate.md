# Delivery estimate per area (v1.63.0)

## Set it up
Online Store > Shop settings > **Delivery areas**. In the **Delivery days** column type *from* and *to* (days after we accept the order; 0 = same day) and press **Save** on the row. Leave both empty for an area to use the estimate of the whole shop (Front page > Estimated delivery).

## What the customer sees
- Checkout: under the area, "Estimated delivery" with dates counted from today.
- Order page and messages: "1 to 2 days after we accept your order".
- Staff see the same line on each delivery order in Online orders.

## Good to know
- The estimate is copied to the order when it is placed; later changes to the area do not change it.
- Pick-up orders have none. The product page shows the shop's estimate only.
- It does not limit the delivery time windows: both are shown.
