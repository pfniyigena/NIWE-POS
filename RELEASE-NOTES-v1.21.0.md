# NIWE ERP, version 1.21.0

Expenses: input VAT, payment accounts, bills with due dates and part-payments, numbers and references, search and filters, several files, delete, bulk approve and reject, monthly budgets, approval rules, an overview and four reports. Versions 1.0.0 to 1.20.0 stay the base. One migration (V60). Four new pages (*Expenses > Overview*, *Budgets*, *Reports*, *Settings*), added by `tools/wire-v210.py`.

## What changed

1. **VAT on an expense.** The expense form has a "VAT included in the total" field (with a calculator that works the VAT out of a total at a rate you type). The total is still what you pay or owe. On approval the category is charged the cost without VAT and the VAT goes to *Input VAT Recoverable*, so it can be claimed back. Voiding reverses both. *Expenses by category* and the other expense reports now show the cost without VAT.
2. **Pay from any account.** *Paid from* offers Cash and any bank or mobile money account in the chart (an asset account that is not inventory, receivables, input VAT, fixed assets and so on). Before, everything came out of Cash. For an unpaid bill it is the account it will usually be paid from; each payment can use another.
3. **Bills: due dates and part-payments.** An unpaid bill gets a due date (typed, or the expense date plus the default days in Settings). On the expense page *Pay this bill* takes an amount, a date, an account and a note, as many times as needed, until the bill is paid off. Each payment posts Dr Accounts Payable, Cr the account paid from, and appears in a Payments table. *Record settlement* still pays the whole rest. A bill that has any payment can no longer be voided (correct it with a journal entry). Bills are marked *Overdue* or *Due soon*; the warning window is set in Settings (default 7 days).
4. **Numbers and references.** Every expense has a number (EXP-000123; existing ones are numbered by the migration) and an optional supplier invoice or receipt reference. Who entered, approved, rejected or voided it is kept.
5. **Search, filters and views.** The list searches payee, memo, number, reference and category, filters by status, category, dates, amount range and branch, and has quick views: Awaiting approval, Unpaid bills, Overdue, Due soon, Entered by me. *Excel* and *PDF* download the register for the dates and status chosen.
6. **Files.** Photos, PDF and text files up to 10 MB, 20 per expense, checked by content. Add them when entering the expense or later on its page (not after it is rejected or voided). The old single receipt picture is still shown for older expenses.
7. **Delete and bulk.** A pending or rejected expense can be deleted (nothing was ever posted for it); approved ones are voided instead. Tick several pending expenses on the list to approve or reject them together (up to 200; those that cannot be done are listed with the reason).
8. **History.** Each expense records every step: created, edited, approved (including an over-budget override), rejected, voided, each payment and each file.
9. **Approval rules** (*Expenses > Settings*, administrators only; all off by default). An approval limit: expenses above it need an administrator or someone given the new *Approve large expenses* permission on their user. A rule that nobody approves, rejects or voids an expense they entered themselves. A block on approving an expense that would take its category or cost center over the monthly budget; an administrator can approve anyway, and it is written to the history.
10. **Budgets.** *Expenses > Budgets* sets a monthly amount per expense category or cost center for a year and shows, for any month, what is spent (approved, without VAT), what is waiting for approval, what is left and a coloured bar (green, amber from 80%, red over). A pending expense that would break its budget shows a warning on its page.
11. **Overview.** *Expenses > Overview*: this month against last, this year, VAT to claim back, awaiting approval, unpaid, overdue and due-soon tiles that open the matching list, the last six months, top categories and vendors, money paid out by account, budgets near or over the limit and the bills that need attention.
12. **Reports** (screen, Excel, PDF, and as scheduled reports): *Expense Register*, *Unpaid Bills by Age*, *Budget vs Actual* and *Expenses by Payment Account*. Staff with Reports or Accounting view access can read them.

## Technical

- Migration `V60__expenses_v121.sql`: `expense` gains number (unique, backfilled), reference, tax amount, paid-from account, due date, amount paid and who entered/approved/rejected/voided; new tables `expense_payment` (existing settled bills get one payment row each), `expense_attachment`, `expense_event`, `expense_budget`, `expense_settings`; the user permission check now also allows `APPROVE_LARGE_EXPENSE`.
- Backend: `ExpenseService` (rewritten), `ExpenseBulkService`, `ExpenseAttachmentService`, `ExpenseBudgetService`, `ExpenseSettingsService`, `ExpenseOverviewService`, `ExpenseReportService`; `AccountingService.postExpense`/`postExpenseVoid` split out VAT and use the paid-from account, new `postExpensePayment`. New endpoints under `/api/accounting/expenses` (list filters, `/summary`, `/payment-accounts`, `/{id}/pay|payments|events|files`, `DELETE /{id}`, `POST /bulk`), `/api/accounting/expense-budgets` (with `/status`), `/api/accounting/expense-settings`; reports `/api/reports/expense-register|expenses-unpaid-aging|expense-budget-vs-actual|expenses-by-payment-account`. The licence gate covers the new paths with the Expenses module. Old callers (real estate commission, work orders, law billing, recurring templates) are unchanged.
- Frontend: new overview, budgets, settings and report-picker pages; rewritten list, form and detail; `expense-api.service`, `models`, `report-defs`, `access` (settings administrators only), user form and list (new permission). `wire-v210.py` adds the four routes and the menu entries.
- Tests: `t110` (API, 288 checks), `walk94` (browser, 71 checks); full regression on the frozen tree.

## Not in this version

Multi-currency expenses, approval chains with several levels, reimbursing staff expense claims through payroll, bank-statement matching, per-month budget amounts (one monthly figure per year for now).
