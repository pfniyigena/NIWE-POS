# Release 1.50.0 - Online Store: product variants (size, colour, model)

- **The shop uses the ERP's own variants.** A product with variants (Products > Variants: a template and one product per size / colour / model, each with its own SKU, price and stock) is shown on the shop as **ONE card**. Nothing is entered twice: put the template on the shop (Shop settings > Products on the shop > search the product > Add) and all its options follow.
- **Product page with option pickers**: one button per value of Colour, Size ... The price, stock, SKU and pictures change with the choice; the cart, the order, the invoice, stock and returns keep working on the chosen option (the line reads "Classic Polo Shirt - Red / M").
- **Each option has its own price**: the online price per option (empty = the option's normal price). Cards say "Starting at X" when the options do not all cost the same; sorting and the price filters use the cheapest option.
- **Pictures**: the product's gallery is shared; every option can also have its own pictures, shown first when the customer chooses it.
- **Sold out options stay visible** (greyed, struck through, "Out of stock") and cannot be added to the cart. A combination that is not sold is skipped: choosing a value moves to the closest option that exists (one in stock first).
- **Reviews and ratings are pooled on the whole product** (one average, one breakdown); each review shows which option was bought. Search, filters, rows (featured, best sellers, offers, new), wishlist, recently viewed, related products and "bought together" count a product once.
- **Staff**: a product with options is one row; "Options: price, stock, pictures" opens the list of options with their SKU, normal price, stock, an online price, a "Sold online" switch, Save and Pictures. The title, description, specifications, tags and the Shown switch belong to the whole product; switching an option off keeps it off when the product is switched back on. New options added in Products > Variants appear on the shop by themselves (at once when the shop list is opened, otherwise within 5 minutes).
- Database: migration V84 adds `online_listing.variant_off`. API: `PublicProduct` gains `variants`, `priceFrom`; the product page returns `options`, `variants`, `selectedVariantId`. Card ids of a product with options are the template's id; any option id also opens the same product page. French texts. See `docs/online-store-variants.md`.

Known limits: at most 3 options and 200 combinations per product (the ERP's own limit); the front-page card picture is the product's own picture (an option's picture shows on the product page); a back-in-stock alert is not part of this release.

Tested in the cloud with a real database and a headless browser: one card with "Starting at", "Choose options", picker states (ok / out / combination not sold), Red/L sold out disables the button, price and SKU follow the choice, add to cart on an option, per-option picture, review pooling (average and variant label), search by SKU and colour, sorts, price and rating filters, order of a sold-out option refused, order of an option accepted (name and stock follow the option), template relisted keeps an option switched off, staff save of option price and option picture upload. Not tested: French screens, phones, the Products > Variants screen after this release's changes, a template with 3 options and 200 combinations.
