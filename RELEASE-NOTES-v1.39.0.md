# Release 1.39.0 - Online Store: abandoned-cart reminders

When a visitor fills a cart, types an e-mail address at checkout and leaves, the shop can now remind them. It is **off until switched on** (Online Store > Shop settings > **Send reminders for abandoned carts**); nothing is saved while it is off.

- **Saved at the e-mail address.** The cart is sent to the shop a moment after the customer types an address that looks right (guests too) or changes the cart. One cart per address. A short notice under the e-mail box tells the customer about the reminders.
- **Two reminders, no discount.** The first about **1 hour** and the second **3 days** after the last change to the cart. The e-mail (in the language the customer used on the shop) lists the cart at today's prices, names what is sold out as no longer available, and has a button **Go back to my cart**; it opens the order form with the cart filled and the e-mail address typed.
- **Nobody is pestered.** An address that orders is not reminded any more; **Stop these reminders** (a link in every e-mail, page `/shop/cart/stop/<token>`) stops them for good and the address is kept as stopped; after a reminder the same address gets at most two reminders in 7 days however often its cart changes; a reminder that would go out more than 2 days late (first) or 10 days late (second) is dropped; a cart with nothing left on sale is dropped without an e-mail; an emptied cart is forgotten. A reminder that fails to send is tried again 30 minutes later, three times at most. Carts untouched for 60 days are deleted (stopped addresses are kept).
- **Staff screen**: **Online Store > Abandoned carts** (`/admin/online-store/carts`): counts (Waiting, Reminded once, Reminded twice, Ordered, Stopped, Nothing to remind), a filterable list with products, pieces, last change and reminder times, the mail server's reason when sending failed, and **Remove** (forgets a cart and its address).
- **Safe**: the order itself is never disturbed (saving the cart answers "ok" whatever happens); saving is limited per connection; a hidden field catches simple robots; a reminder is marked as taken before it is mailed, so a crash can lose one e-mail but never send two at once.

New: migration `V75__online_store_cart_reminders.sql` (tables `online_cart`, `online_cart_line`, column `online_store_settings.cart_reminders_enabled`; runs by itself at start-up), `CartReminderService`, `CartReminderJob` (every 3 minutes), public endpoints `PUT /api/public/store/carts`, `GET /api/public/store/carts/{token}`, `POST /api/public/store/carts/stop`, staff endpoints `GET /api/online-store/carts` and `DELETE /api/online-store/carts/{id}`, optional setting `app.online-store.cart-reminders.batch-size` (default 20 per pass), French texts (screens and server). Not included: a discount, a third reminder, text-message reminders, the value of the cart in the staff list.

Checked live on the real application started on an empty PostgreSQL (all 75 migrations, `ddl-auto=validate`), with a fake mail server that speaks STARTTLS: saving off/on, address normalisation and rejection of bad addresses, robot field, unknown products, 31 lines refused; unchanged re-save keeps the time while a changed cart moves it; the first reminder (French) and second reminder e-mails with the right products, prices and links; sold-out only cart, ordered cart, too-late cart, stopped cart and emptied cart each get no e-mail and the right state; the restore link returns today's cart; the stop link; deletion of 70-day-old carts while a stopped address stays; the staff list, filter, permission check and delete. The Angular screen files were checked with a strict compile. Not run in a browser, and not tried against a real mail server.
