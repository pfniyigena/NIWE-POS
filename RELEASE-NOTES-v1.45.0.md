# Release 1.45.0 - Online Store: shop experience

A round of improvements to how customers browse and choose. Built now: product cards, search and filters, product page extras, category tiles and the shop's own colour. Left for later (asked): wishlist, product variants (size, colour), bank card payments.

- **Product cards**: same height and picture shape everywhere, name on at most two lines, big price with the normal price struck through, a **-NN%** badge on offers, stars with the number of reviews (or "No reviews yet"), **In stock / Few left / Out of stock**, a **Quick view** window (picture, price, stock, short description, add to cart, link to the full page) and an Add to cart button always at the bottom of the card.
- **Search**: every word typed must be found in the name, web title, SKU, barcode, brand, category or description, in any order, so "hose garden" finds "20m Garden Hose". **Suggestions** appear while typing (products, categories, brands).
- **Filters and sorting**: price range (as the customer sees it, tax included), brand, rating (4, 3 or 2 stars and up), in-stock only; sort by recommended, newest, most popular (units sold on online orders), best rated, price low to high or high to low. "Clear filters" resets. The front page rows step aside while a filter is on.
- **Recently viewed**: the last products a visitor opened, kept in the visitor's own browser only; shown on the front page and on product pages.
- **Product page**: hover zoom and a full-screen picture view (arrows, Escape), a quantity selector, a Delivery box (cheapest delivery fee, free-delivery threshold, pick-up address and an **estimated delivery date range**), **Share on WhatsApp**, and **Frequently bought together** (products that most often share an online order with this one, with "Add all to cart").
- **Home page**: **Shop by category** tiles (each shows a picture of one of its products and the number of products).
- **Shop colour and delivery days** (staff, Shop settings > Front page): a colour for buttons and the announcement bar, and the estimated delivery time in days (for example 2 to 4). The date shown to customers is counted from today, as if the order were accepted today.
- Migration V80 (three columns on online_store_settings). French texts.

Known limits: price sort and range use the shop price with tax added when the tax is on top; "most popular" counts units on online orders that were not rejected or cancelled; estimated dates count calendar days; one shop colour, no separate dark-mode colour.

Tested on PostgreSQL through the API (multi-word search, SKU/brand search, every filter and sort, suggestions, recent ids, bought-together from real orders, colour and delivery-day validation) and in a headless browser (card heights and button alignment, badge, Quick view, filters, suggestions opening a product, tiles, zoom, lightbox, quantity selector, delivery box, add all, recently viewed, shop colour on buttons, phone width without sideways scrolling, staff fields). Not yet checked: French screens, the real Maven build, `ng build --configuration store`, a large catalogue's search speed (100,000 products: the description search is a LIKE scan and may need an index or a search engine then).
