# Release 1.51.0 - Online Store: contact form and information pages

- **Contact form** at `/shop/info/contact` (linked in the shop footer as "Contact us"): the shop's phone, e-mail, WhatsApp, address and opening hours, and a form (name, e-mail, phone, subject, message, optional order code; the order code is filled in when the link carries `?order=CODE`). The visitor must give an e-mail or a phone number so the shop can answer. A hidden field catches robots and one connection can send 5 messages an hour.
- **Online Store > Messages** (staff): every message with its sender, contact details and order code; tabs New / Answered / Everything with search; "Reply by e-mail" and "WhatsApp" buttons, a note and "Mark as answered" (or "Mark as new" again), Delete. The shop is told of each new message by e-mail (and by SMS when the owner alert is set up), the visitor who gave an e-mail gets a confirmation with a copy of the message in their language. The number of new messages is returned by `GET /api/online-store/orders/pending-count` as `messagesNew`.
- **Four information pages**: Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Returns & Refunds, Delivery & Shipping, at `/shop/info/terms|privacy|returns|delivery`, linked in the footer. Each starts with a **general starter text in English and French** (the visitor's language), with the shop's name, phone, e-mail and address filled in. **Staff must read and adapt these texts; they are not legal advice.**
- **Online Store > Shop pages** (staff): edit the title and text of each page (paragraphs; `## ` heading; `- ` bullet; `{shop}`, `{phone}`, `{email}`, `{address}` are replaced by the shop's details), switch a page off, go back to the starter text. A page staff saved is shown as written to every visitor (one language); until then the visitor sees the starter text in their language.
- **Checkout** says "By placing your order you agree to Terms & Conditions, Returns & Refunds" with links (only the pages that are shown). No tick box: the sentence is information, as on most shops.
- Database: migration V85 (`online_info_page`, `online_contact_message`). API: public `GET /api/public/store/info`, `GET /api/public/store/info/{slug}?lang=`, `POST /api/public/store/contact`; staff `GET/PUT /api/online-store/info-pages`, `POST .../restore`, `GET /api/online-store/messages`, `POST .../answered`, `.../reopen`, `DELETE`. French texts. See `docs/online-store-pages-contact.md`.

Known limits: staff cannot reply from inside the ERP (the reply button opens the e-mail program or WhatsApp); a page staff wrote exists in one language only (a second language is not built); no attachments in the contact form; messages are kept until staff delete them.

Tested in the cloud with a real database and a headless browser: footer links, checkout sentence, the four pages (English and French API), unknown page, form checks (name, e-mail or phone, message), sending with an order code from the link, robot field ignored, message in the staff list with e-mail link, mark answered with a note, answered tab, editing a page (title, headings, bullets, shop name filled in), restore, switching a page off hides it from the footer and the page itself (404), the New count. Not tested: real e-mail delivery of the alert and the confirmation, French screens, phones.
