# Release 1.40.0 - Online Store: loyalty points

Signed-in shop customers now earn **loyalty points** on completed orders and spend them at checkout. It is **off until switched on** (Online Store > Shop settings > **Customers earn and spend loyalty points**; Customer accounts must be on). These points belong to shop accounts and are **separate from the till's loyalty programme** in Sales (that programme is untouched).

- **Earning**: when an order is **completed**, the customer earns **1 point per 100** paid for the goods by default (setting 1 to 100), counted after any coupon or points discount and without the delivery fee. The "order complete" e-mail says how many points were earned (French too); the order page shows it.
- **Spending**: at checkout a signed-in customer with points sees **Use loyalty points** (a number box and **Use the most I can**). **1 point = 1 unit of the currency**, and points may pay at most a share of the goods left after a coupon (setting 0 to 100%, default 50%). The server decides how many points are really used (balance and share) and locks the account while the order is saved, so the same points can never be spent twice. The points discount is spread over the order lines like a coupon, so the sale order, invoice, returns and reports stay consistent (invoice = what the customer paid). Guests earn and spend nothing. Points do not expire.
- **Cancelled, refused or unpaid orders** (customer or staff cancel, refusal, "not paid in time") give the spent points back, once. **Returned goods**: the matching share of the points spent on them is given back and the matching share of the points they earned is taken back, never more than the order spent or earned (the balance may go below zero if the customer already spent them; points cannot be spent while it is below zero).
- **History**: every change is a row (earned, used, returned, taken back, adjusted) with the balance after it, shown to the customer in the shop's 👤 panel and to staff.
- **Staff screen**: **Online Store > Loyalty points** (`/admin/online-store/loyalty`): customers with their points (most first, search by name, e-mail or phone), the history of one customer, and **add or remove points** with a reason (needs the Operations manage right; the balance cannot go below zero). The staff order screen shows points used and earned.
- **Reports**: the online sales reports count the points discount in the order's discount; the **Coupons** report counts only the coupon part.
- Deleting a customer's account deletes their points history.

New: migration `V76__online_store_loyalty.sql` (table `shop_loyalty_entry` with unique rows so an order earns, spends and gives back once and a return restores and takes back once; columns on `shop_customer`, `online_order`, `online_store_settings`; runs by itself at start-up), `ShopLoyaltyService`, endpoints `GET /api/public/store/account/loyalty` and (staff) `GET /api/online-store/loyalty`, `GET /api/online-store/loyalty/{id}`, `POST /api/online-store/loyalty/{id}/adjust`, `pointsToUse` on placing an order, French texts (screens and server). Not included: expiry, points for guests, bonus or multiplier offers, points on the delivery fee, sharing points with the till.

Checked live on the real application started on PostgreSQL over the previous database (migration V76 applied on top of existing data): sign-in, earn on completion (177 points on 17,700), spend capped by balance and share, cancel gives back once, a second cancel does nothing, guest and "off" ignore points, invoice total equals the order total, a 5-of-10 return gave back 50 and took back 88 points and a repeated process was refused, staff list, search, history, adjust (zero, empty reason and below-zero refused), a coupon and points together (invoice 15,705 = 17,700 - 1,770 - 225), the coupon report counting only the coupon part, and the points e-mail. The Angular screen files were checked with a strict compile. Not run in a browser, and not tried against a real mail server.
