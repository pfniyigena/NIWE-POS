# Release 1.54.0 - Online Store: back-in-stock alerts

- **"Tell me when it is back"**: on the product page, a sold-out product (or a sold-out option such as Red / M) shows a small box: the customer leaves an e-mail address and/or a phone number and presses "Tell me". The request is kept for that exact product or option. Asking twice with the same e-mail or number does nothing new; if the product can be bought meanwhile, the page says so.
- **The shop tells them by itself**: every five minutes the shop looks at the waiting requests. As soon as a product can be sold again (stock is back, after the stock buffer and reservations), each customer gets **one** e-mail in their language ("{product} is back in stock", with a link to it) and, when the shop sends text messages (Shop settings > Text messages), one text message. The request is then closed: nobody is told twice. If the e-mail or text cannot be sent, it is tried three times, then marked "Could not be told". Customers whose only contact is a phone number while text messages are off stay Waiting for staff.
- **Online Store > Waiting customers** (new menu entry): the products customers are waiting for, the most wanted first (what to restock), with "back in stock" shown when a product can be sold again; the list of people (Waiting / Told / Could not be told / Everything, search); a **WhatsApp** button per person with the message and product link already written; **Mark as told** (when you called or wrote yourself); **Remove**; and **Tell them now** to run the notice immediately instead of waiting for the next pass.
- Protection: 10 requests an hour per connection, a hidden field catches robots, an e-mail address is checked.
- Database: migration V88 (`online_stock_alert`). API: public `POST /api/public/store/stock-alerts`; staff `GET /api/online-store/stock-alerts[/demand]`, `POST .../stock-alerts/notify`, `POST .../stock-alerts/{id}/told`, `DELETE .../stock-alerts/{id}`. Permissions: view / manage like the orders. French texts.

Tested in the cloud (PostgreSQL, real server, a mail server with STARTTLS and login, headless browser): in-stock product refused politely; bad e-mail, no contact, robot field, unknown and template ids; duplicate request; two customers on one product; demand list; no message while still sold out; one e-mail with the right subject and link once stock came back and none on the second pass; a failing mail server (three tries then Could not be told); mark as told; remove; the product page box on a sold-out option (errors, thank-you, not shown on an in-stock product); the staff screen (chips, filter by product, WhatsApp link, tabs). Not tested: a real text provider, French screens, a real mail host.
