# Release 1.56.0 - Online Store: order reception (code, signature, "I received it")

An order that is out for delivery or ready for pick-up can now be **completed by the person who receives it**. Three ways, all of them do what "Complete" did (stock out, invoice, e-mail with the invoice, loyalty points) and, for an order paid in cash, **record the cash as collected**:

- **The customer taps "I received my order"** on the order page (and from My orders in the portal), with an optional signature drawn with a finger or the mouse. Proof that the order is theirs: the account that holds it, or the phone number of the order.
- **The delivery code**: when the order goes out (or is ready) the customer gets a 4-digit code in the e-mail / text message, in the portal (My orders) and on the order page. The driver, or the staff at the counter, types the code the customer reads out; it completes the order. The code is never shown to staff or drivers and is not on the delivery slip. After five wrong codes the code is blocked for half an hour (a signature still works).
- **The customer signs on the driver's phone**: the new screen **Online Store > My deliveries** (made for a phone) lists the orders out for delivery (pick your name once; it is remembered) with the customer's phone (tap to call), the address (map link), the goods and what to collect, and **Hand over**: the code, or a signature box. 

Also: the staff Orders screen has a box on every order that is out or ready (type the code, or have the customer sign); a completed order shows how it was received (customer / code / signature) and **View signature**; the **delivery slip** prints "Received on ..." with the signature picture, or asks the driver for the code; the order history says who confirmed. The staff user's name is kept as "received by" (the customer's own tap is recorded as "customer").

- Cash: an order paid on delivery is recorded as paid in cash when it is completed this way. Because a customer's tap does not prove the driver collected the money, check the driver's cash against the run sheet as before.
- Database: migration V89 (delivery code, wrong-code counter, received at / via / by, signed name, signature picture on `online_order`). Orders that went out before this version get their code when the customer first looks for it.
- API: public `POST /api/public/store/orders/{code}/receipt` (the code, with phone or account) and `.../received` (the tap); staff `POST /api/online-store/orders/{id}/receive` and `GET .../signature`. Account orders carry `receiptCode` and `canConfirm`. French texts.

Tested in the cloud (PostgreSQL, real server, mail server, headless browser, phone size): the code shown to the signed-in customer and to a guest by phone (wrong phone refused), never in the staff or driver data; nothing given / bad picture refused; five wrong codes then locked, and unlocked after half an hour; completion by the customer's tap with a signature, by code, and by driver signature, each with the invoice, the cash payment and the history line; a second tap refused; the signature picture and the slip with the signature; the staff Orders box; the driver screen (pick a driver, signature, wrong then right code, list empties). Not tested: a real text provider, French screens, a real phone's touch drawing.
