# NIWE ERP, version 1.20.0

Help Desk: deadlines (SLA), waiting on the customer, replies by e-mail, saved replies, files, customer replies and ratings on the public page, overview, reports, views, bulk actions, history, tags, links, and merging. Versions 1.0.0 to 1.19.0 stay the base. One migration (V59). Two new pages (*Help Desk > Overview* and *Help Desk > Saved replies*), added by `tools/wire-v200.py`.

## What changed

1. **Deadlines (SLA).** *Help Desk > Settings* has a Deadlines section: switch it on and set, per priority, the hours for a first reply and for resolution (defaults: urgent 2/8, high 8/48, medium 24/120, low 72/240). Every new customer ticket gets both deadlines (internal tickets too if you tick that box). Hours are calendar hours. Tickets created before the switch was turned on have no deadlines. Changing a ticket's priority recalculates them. The list and the ticket show *on track*, *due soon*, *overdue*, *met* or *was late*.
2. **Waiting for the customer.** A new status. While a ticket waits, its deadline clock stands still: when work resumes the deadlines move later by the time waited. You can put a ticket on hold with *Wait for customer*, or tick *Then wait for the customer's answer* when sending a reply. A customer reply on the public page puts it back to work.
3. **Escalation e-mails (off by default).** When on, the people on the notification list and the assigned agent get one e-mail when the first-reply deadline is missed, one warning a set number of hours before the resolution deadline (default 4) and one when it is missed. A check runs every five minutes; each deadline is reported once.
4. **Auto-close (off by default).** Resolved tickets close by themselves after a chosen number of days.
5. **Replies and notes.** The ticket page separates *Internal note* from *Reply to the customer*. A reply is e-mailed to the requester when the ticket has an address (switchable in Settings), with the subject "Re: <subject> [<ticket number>]". The first reply (or resolving) stops the first-reply clock.
6. **Saved replies.** *Help Desk > Saved replies* keeps ready-made answers (optionally for one category) with {{requester}}, {{ticket}}, {{subject}} and {{agent}} filled in when used from the ticket page.
7. **Files.** Pictures, PDF and text files up to 10 MB, 20 per ticket, checked by content not just by name. Staff files are private unless you tick *Customer can see*; customers can add files on the public page.
8. **Public ticket page.** After looking up a ticket the customer sees the replies meant for them (never notes or staff names), can reply (reopens a resolved ticket), add files, and rate the help from 1 to 5 once it is resolved or closed. Replying and rating can each be switched off. The assigned agent (or the notification list) is e-mailed when the customer replies.
9. **Overview.** *Help Desk > Overview*: unanswered, overdue, due soon and unassigned tiles that open the matching list, open/in progress/waiting counts, tickets in and out over 7 days, deadline compliance, average first reply and resolution time (waiting excluded), average rating, a priority and age breakdown, workload per agent and a needs-attention list.
10. **Views, tags and bulk actions.** The ticket list has views (Active, Mine, Unassigned, Unanswered, Overdue, Due soon, Waiting, All), a tag filter, search that also finds e-mail addresses and tags, and a bulk bar: assign, set priority, add a tag, start, wait for customer, close (up to 200 at once; tickets that cannot take the action are skipped and listed with the reason).
11. **History, links, merge, rating.** Every step is recorded with who and when. A ticket can be linked to an invoice and a job (work order) by number, tagged, and merged into another open ticket (its conversation and files move across and it is closed as *Merged*). The customer's rating and comment show on the ticket.
12. **Reports.** *Help Desk Deadlines (SLA)*, *Help Desk by Agent*, *Help Desk by Category*, *Help Desk Satisfaction* (date range) and *Overdue Help Desk Tickets*, each as screen, Excel and PDF, and as scheduled reports. Staff with operations view access can read them.

## Technical

- Migration `V59__helpdesk_v120.sql`: status check now OPEN, IN_PROGRESS, WAITING, RESOLVED, CLOSED, MERGED; new deadline, waiting, tag, link, merge and rating columns on `help_desk_ticket`; `help_desk_comment` gains `from_requester` and `emailed` (author optional); new settings columns; new tables `help_desk_event`, `help_desk_attachment`, `help_desk_canned_response`.
- Backend: `HelpDeskTicketService` (rewritten), `HelpDeskSla`, `HelpDeskSlaService` (scheduled every five minutes; `POST /api/helpdesk/tickets/sla/run` for administrators), `HelpDeskWorkflowService`, `HelpDeskOverviewService`, `HelpDeskAttachmentService`, `HelpDeskCannedService`, `HelpDeskEvents`, `HelpDeskMailService`, `HelpDeskReportService`. New endpoints: tickets `?view=&tag=`, `GET /summary`, `POST /{id}/wait|merge`, `PUT /{id}/tags|links`, `GET /{id}/events`, attachments under `/{id}/attachments`, `POST /bulk`, `/api/helpdesk/canned`; public `POST /api/public/helpdesk/tickets/reply|rate|attachments`, `GET /tickets/attachments/{id}`; reports `/api/reports/helpdesk-sla|helpdesk-by-agent|helpdesk-by-category|helpdesk-satisfaction|helpdesk-overdue`. Reads need operations view, writes operations manage, settings administrator.
- Frontend: new `helpdesk-overview`, `helpdesk-canned-list`, `helpdesk-ui`; rewritten ticket list, ticket detail, settings and public page; `report-defs`, API services and models. `wire-v200.py` adds the two routes, the two menu entries and opens the Help Desk menu group on its own pages.
- Tests: `t109` (API, 253 checks), `walk93` (browser, 69 checks); full regression on the frozen tree.

## Not in this version

Business-hours calendars, e-mail-to-ticket (creating tickets from incoming mail), live collision warnings when two agents open one ticket, customer accounts for the public page.
